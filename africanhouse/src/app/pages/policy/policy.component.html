Customer & Shopping Policy

AfriLoom Fabrics — Every Fabric Tells a Story.

Effective Date: DECEMBER 2026

At AfriLoom Fabrics, we want every customer to shop with confidence. Because our fabrics are sold online, we are committed to providing accurate product information, clear pricing, secure payment options and reliable delivery.

1. Product Information

We make every effort to ensure that our product photos, descriptions, colours and measurements accurately represent the fabrics we sell.

However, colours may appear slightly different depending on your device screen, lighting and photography conditions.

Customers are encouraged to review the product photos, description, available yards and minimum order quantity before placing an order.

2. Minimum Yard Purchase

Each fabric has a stated minimum number of yards that can be purchased.

For example, if a fabric has a minimum purchase of 4 yards, customers can purchase 4 yards → 8 yards → 12 yards → 16 yards, depending on availability. Customers cannot purchase more yards than the quantity currently available.

3. Availability & Stock

Our website displays the available stock for each fabric. Stock is subject to availability and may sell out quickly. Once the available quantity reaches the minimum order quantity or falls below it, the fabric may automatically be marked Sold Out. We will not knowingly accept orders for fabric that is unavailable.

4. Orders & Payment

An order is considered confirmed only after successful payment has been received. Customers should carefully review their fabric selection, quantity, name, phone number, delivery address and order total before completing payment. Once an order has been confirmed, changes may not be possible if the fabric has already been prepared for delivery.

5. Delivery

AfriLoom Fabrics offers delivery within Ghana. Delivery charges depend on the customer's location and the applicable delivery arrangement.

Free delivery within Accra applies to orders of GH₵600 or more, subject to the terms of any active promotion.

Customers are responsible for providing a correct and complete delivery address and reachable telephone number. If a delivery cannot be completed because the customer provided an incorrect address, unavailable phone number or is unavailable to receive the order, any additional delivery cost may be charged to the customer.

6. Returns & Exchanges

We want you to be happy with your purchase. Because fabrics are sold by the yard and may be cut specifically according to the customer's order, cut fabric cannot be returned because the customer has changed their mind, selected the wrong fabric, colour or quantity, or no longer wants the item.

However, we will make things right when the mistake is ours. You may contact us if:

  • You received a different fabric from the one you ordered.
  • You received the wrong quantity.
  • The fabric has a significant manufacturing defect.
  • The fabric was damaged before or during delivery.
  • Your order contains an error caused by AfriLoom Fabrics.

To qualify for a return/exchange: Please contact us within 24 hours of receiving your order and provide your order number, clear photos/videos of the fabric, a description of the problem, and photos of the packaging where relevant. Please do not cut, sew, wash, alter or otherwise use the fabric before reporting an issue.

If the issue is confirmed to be our responsibility, AfriLoom Fabrics will offer a replacement, exchange, or refund where appropriate.

7. Refunds

If AfriLoom Fabrics approves a refund, it will be made using the appropriate payment method or another agreed method. The time for funds to appear may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

We will not issue refunds for:

  • Change of mind after the fabric has been cut.
  • Incorrect fabric or quantity selected by the customer.
  • Fabric that has been cut, sewn, washed, altered or damaged after delivery.
  • Failure to inspect the order within the stated reporting period.

These exclusions do not remove your right to contact us if the problem was caused by AfriLoom Fabrics.

8. Order Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation before the order has been processed or prepared for dispatch. Once an order has been cut, packaged or handed over for delivery, cancellation may no longer be possible. If AfriLoom cancels an order because the fabric is unavailable, the customer will be entitled to an appropriate refund.

9. Delivery Inspection

We strongly recommend that customers check their order as soon as it is received. Please confirm the fabric is correct, the quantity is correct, there is no obvious damage, and the fabric matches your order. If there is an issue, contact us within 24 hours.

10. Customer Privacy

AfriLoom Fabrics respects your privacy. Information such as your name, phone number, delivery address and order details may be collected to process your order, arrange delivery, contact you regarding your order, provide customer support and maintain transaction records.

We will take reasonable steps to protect customer information and will not sell your personal information. Where personal information is shared with a service provider, it will be shared only as necessary to provide the relevant service or as otherwise permitted or required by law, in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act.

11. Customer Support

We value every customer and want to resolve problems fairly. For questions, order issues or complaints, please contact us:

  • WhatsApp 0240 070 628
  • Instagram @afriloom_fabrics
  • TikTok @afriloom_fabrics
  • Snapchat @afriloom_fab

We will make reasonable efforts to respond to customer enquiries and resolve genuine issues promptly.

At AfriLoom Fabrics, we believe every fabric tells a story — and every customer deserves a trustworthy shopping experience. We are committed to honest product descriptions, transparent pricing, secure payments, careful packaging, reliable delivery and fair customer service.
